# Durable research record

Use an ignored study directory under the canonical `runs/`, for example `runs/probe-research/<question-id>/`. These are record responsibilities, not a new database, CLI or scheduler. Reuse existing manifests, raw records and immutable references. The core probe skill's registry/study shape remains the viewer interface.

| Record | Minimum contents |
|---|---|
| Question/contract | Outcome and estimand; launch and conditional-native scope; incumbent; complete benefit/regression rule; power/alpha and practical effect; source-family/cell counts; finite search ceiling, protected confirmation/native reserves and terminal states. |
| Sources/exposure | Declared corpus, pins and context/save provenance; family/fork membership; every recoverable earned milestone-success window and matched failures; inherited/automatic/human assistance; readout/tool eras; private or missing reasoning; full-read coverage/gaps; protected metadata versus exposed development families across studies. |
| Strategy map | Plausible mechanisms and predicted behavior; screened, ruled-out, deferred and unexplored branches; evidence, costs and reason to narrow or open an escape branch. |
| Candidates/portfolio | Immutable IDs/hashes, parents, exact template/modules and rendered inputs; placement/context/memory policy; task/actor/action ownership; eligibility and activation/suspension/resumption/replanning/retirement; helpful components, regressions and proposed discriminating tests. Combinations identify constituents and predicted interactions. |
| Batches | Frozen arms/cases/families, count units and independent plan/seed identities; model/runtime/sampling/scorer hashes, actual caller/wire/fit proof and corrected-replay bridge where needed; balanced schedule, publication ID, full cost and start/terminal receipts. |
| Attempts/samples | Unique job/attempt IDs journaled before calls, exact input references, raw response and every exposed thinking block, actual calls/results, timestamps/errors and locks. Preserve failed/uncertain attempts; missing executor/private thinking remains unavailable. |
| Trace audit | Per-arm/case/stage codebook and evidence; recognition, stated task, actor, intent, actual request, legality/native transition and continuity/retirement distinguished; uncertainty/disagreement; blinded grade locks versus posthoc explanations; complete reading coverage. |
| Resource/resume state | Aggregate cap and grant semantics (inclusive versus additional), spent/uncertain/reserved time without double counting; physical devices, leases/owners/windows; planner/executor and fit costs; protected reserves; next executable decision and unbegun positions. A new phase, agent or restart does not reset the question. |
| Native handoff | Evidence gate and separately authorized fixed comparison; save/context/task-state restoration proof or explicit state-only scope; target flags false, actual treatment delivery, native predicate/stop rule and retirement follow-up; independent family versus conditional trial interpretation. |
| Decisions/result | Completed-batch evidence and trace-supported next decision; portfolio versus one frozen finalist, component/combination lineage, effects/uncertainty and each acceptance check; nulls/harms/invalidations, full search cost and exact supported/rejected/inconclusive scope. |

Statuses should distinguish design infeasibility, waiting for compute, incomplete sampling, invalid comparison, grading/integrity blockage, supported improvement, rejection and inconclusive evidence. The remaining cap, reserve, source/data identity, exposure and resume action must be recoverable without chat memory. Release owned compute before offline grading or waiting.

Before inference, a GPU-free forward rehearsal should exercise the actual relevant runner's budget exhaustion, failed acquisition, uncertain/interrupted calls, immutable resume without resampling, failed grading and missing confirmation/checkpoint context. Independent skill evaluators should receive realistic requests and raw fixtures without the intended answer; assess their decisions and artifacts, not whether they repeat headings. Fixtures establish workflow handling, never measured samples, power evidence or a registered real lane.
