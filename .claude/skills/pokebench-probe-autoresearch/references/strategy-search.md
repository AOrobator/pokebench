# Search the strategy space

Read before choosing the first exploratory batch and again when deciding whether to narrow, combine or leave a stalled branch. The core [decision-probe skill](../../pokebench-decision-probes/SKILL.md) owns qualification, grading, compute and confirmation. This reference guides which hypotheses earn those resources.

## Map before narrowing

Write a compact strategy map from the actual failure. Useful dimensions include evidence delivery, task priority, context/history, working memory, action binding, preparation, planner/executor organization and task persistence. For each plausible mechanism record:

- Trace evidence and the predicted change in behavior.
- A distinct candidate or an offline reason it cannot explain the failure.
- Status: screened, ruled out, deferred or unexplored; cost and the next discriminating test.

Use model cards/prompting guidance, harness research, prior local studies, game source/walkthroughs and human or speedrun records where relevant. Record source/version, applicability and negative findings. Research can identify a hypothesis without authorizing an unobserved walkthrough fact in the model's prompt. Check earlier failed variants before proposing the same intervention under a new name.

Choose enough meaningfully different branches to distinguish the plausible explanations. Six arms is not a universal requirement. A narrow question or budget can justify fewer, but record why. At each adaptation, compare improving the leading branch with testing another explanation. Allocate a bounded opportunity for an escape branch; do not spend the whole search on cosmetic synonyms after the first favorable result.

## Learn from actual successes

Declare the research corpus and sealed confirmation reserve before reading outcomes. Within that corpus, inventory every recoverable earned success at the target milestone and inspect its relevant decision windows alongside matched failures. Deduplicate copied/forked windows; disclose unavailable records and which material was read in full. Do not open sealed confirmation outcomes to complete a research census; if the user requires their inspection, mark the families exposed and replace the reserve before a transfer claim.

Use native evidence when a viewer misses progress. Separate earned transitions from inherited saves, automatic harness actions, human/parent help and model-selected actions. Compare tools, readout era, context, budgets and assistance before attributing a difference to model identity. A successful outcome with a false rationale or an automatic interaction is not a template for successful model thinking. Unavailable private reasoning stays unavailable.

## Keep a component portfolio

Retain a small, budgeted portfolio of promising mechanisms, not only one total-score winner. A candidate may improve task choice while failing action binding; another may improve execution but lose the episode. Record their distinct strengths, failures, regressions, latency and evidence scope. Better thinking may justify a component test, never promotion or an imagined executor success.

Candidate identity includes exact prompt modules and their placement, context/memory policy, eligibility, task/actor/action ownership, replanning conditions and suspension/resumption/retirement. Pin tools, model/sampling and evaluator unless a separately declared contrast changes them. Preserve parent/version lineage and the trace evidence motivating each revision. A new architecture can be informative even when no wording change helps.

Before a combination, state the complementary mechanism and check conflicts: competing task owners, contradictory instructions, lost observations, duplicate prompts and context/output budgets. Compare with the relevant freshly sampled parent and constituents using a matched ablation or a feasible factorial under the authorized design. Individually positive components need not add; a compound candidate does not identify which sentence caused an effect.

Choose between revising, replicating, branching, combining, freezing a finalist or stopping only after the complete batch and its [trace audit](trace-analysis.md). Record the next decision and why it is more informative than another wording sweep. Respect the campaign's finite revision ceiling and protected validation reserves; an honest null or infeasibility result is a valid terminal outcome.
