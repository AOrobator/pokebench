Prompt selection, delivery and revision

Freeze original model artifact/quantization, context, sampling, effective harness vector, tool schemas and base prompt. Candidate delivery requires an independently registered default-OFF switch, production OFF byte-parity tests, case eligibility proof, exact one-shot model-delivery receipt and normal compaction preservation contract; code implementation and review remain future work. Use the same existing supervisor/guard policy in all arms; it may not inject candidate-specific coaching. Verify productive training is not falsely stopped. Baseline receives no added candidate text. Record input tokens/context length, every compaction, and whether the one-shot reminder is still present or faithfully retained. Do not silently reinsert it if compaction drops it; delivery failure is evidence. Treatment adds one clearly delimited reminder at the selected post-loss decision, with no additional recurring insertion. History retains it under the same compaction policy. Prompt delivery is part of the treatment; persistent/repeated variants would need another campaign.

Candidate A mechanism: distinguish the story goal from the model's earlier tactical plan and evaluate whether the present capabilities differ from those that lost. Candidate B mechanism: maintain a short, observation-grounded preparation goal while choosing only executable current-map actions. These are hypotheses, not exact frozen prompts yet. Before launch write exact text, eligibility, length cap (suggested≤180words), factual premises, reference run/seq, parent and falsifying observation. No routes, encounter locations, species recommendations or desired move choice imported from the successful trace. Any supplemental game fact must be game-owned, source-verified and previously observed in that case; no new factual lane in this campaign.

Initial exact-text proposals (freeze after applicability checks, before launch): A — loss comparison: “Your goal is to make story progress. Compare the party you have now with the party that started the last battle you lost: Pokémon, levels, evolution and known moves. Restoring HP after a full-health loss does not change those capabilities. Any earlier strategy you proposed is your own plan and may be revised. Use the observed evidence to choose your next strategy and act on it.” B — preparation continuity: “After a loss, use observed facts to decide whether you need different capabilities before another attempt. If you choose preparation, choose a concrete goal and carry it through across turns; check the game's actual results before deciding you are ready to return. Use actions supported by your current location and tool instructions. You choose the strategy, destination and actions.” These prompts name no route, species, move choice or particular training method. They are candidates, not validated wording.

Batch1 freezes baseline+A+B on both development families:6episodes, one draw per arm/family. Randomize balanced arm/family order and counterbalance device allocation. Inspect only when all6terminate. Trace diagnosis covers unwillingness, unsupported facts, current-map tool errors, goal abandonment, lost gains, move/evolution handling, premature retry and justified ready-team retry. Every causal explanation is a hypothesis.

Batch2 freezes fresh baseline+one exact treatment on both development families:4episodes. Select parent by higher strict recovery count, then fewer unchanged rematches, then more native wins, then fewer tokens, then stable candidate id. If one evidenced local failure admits a focused correction, make one revision of that parent. If parent succeeds in both families, replicate it unchanged. If both mechanisms fail and no source-grounded focused revision exists, stop instead of inventing a third mechanism. Revision ledger records parent, exact wording delta, cited run/seq and prediction. Maximum3sampled treatment versions (A,B,one revision), with one revision round.

Batch3 freezes baseline+current finalist on one development family, chosen before batch1 by largest unchanged-loss burden, tie source id:2episodes. Nomination requires the exact finalist to have achieved strict recovery in both development families in its own sampled episodes, a strict-recovery rate at least25percentage points above the equal-weight family mean of its directly matched baseline episodes, with equal candidate/control denominators in each included batch, and a successful final replication; no inherited parent results or factual-error-driven gain. A revision receives both families in batch2, so it can qualify without weakening the rule. Before batch3 require both-family strict recovery, the matched25percentage-point lift and no factual-error-driven gain. If no candidate clears those preliminary gates, stop. Final nomination additionally requires the successful batch3 replication; do not apply that requirement before replication occurs. At most one nominee. All exploratory comparisons are directional, not significant winner claims.

Full-trace scoring and immutable evidence

Primary recovery outcome: within the cap, actually leave the gym from the qualified inside-gym episode start, acquire a qualifying native capability change through model-selected play, and return to start a Roxanne battle with that change, with no earlier unchanged-capability rematch in the episode. Freeze this strict endpoint so a bad retry cannot be erased by eventual recovery. Also report eventual recovery after bad retries separately. Gym doorway entry alone is a diagnostic, not the battle-return endpoint.

Compare party by stable identities, not slot order, against both episode start and the last comparable full-health losing battle start. Qualifying changes: same Pokémon higher level; new species/party member; evolution; changed known moveset. Count these loose changes even if weak; annotate move removal/replacement and relevance separately. Party reorder, nickname, HP/PP restoration after a full-health loss, EXP short of a level, and bag-only purchases do not meet this endpoint. Removed party member alone is reported separately rather than treated as acquisition. User-approved loose capability definitions must be explicit before the batch; no grader discretion to redefine after observing results.

Required secondary: native ROXANNE_DEFEATED by the cap. Also: actual exit; attempts to train/catch; completed gains; return; number of unchanged rematches; gym entries; useful/irrelevant changes; turns/tokens/time to recovery and victory; trainer/wild battles; errors; terminal reasons. Failures are censored at cap; never report survivor-only speed as overall performance. A preparation success with no victory is recovery evidence only.

Readiness-control outcome: actually initiates the justified Roxanne rematch within10minutes/70decisions, retaining the qualified improvement; preparation intent or leaving the gym does not pass. Both control identities and eligibility are frozen before any campaign sampling. Nominee must pass all4control episodes and have no candidate-only failure where its matched baseline succeeds. Tiny control counts do not prove noninferiority.

The recovery sequence is a campaign-specific native-state-derived diagnostic endpoint, not a registered story milestone or frontier credit. Native event/state extraction supplies maps, party snapshots, actions and outcomes. Two independent blinded trace reviews check recovery sequence, readiness, false premises and usability. A lossless grading projection removes only the inserted reminder and explicit version labels, preserving all subsequent model behavior, tool outputs, frames and native state; full original traces remain immutable with a mask manifest. Reviewers never see the original intervention-bearing request snapshots. Pseudonymous episode ids hide prompt/version and scores; actions can reveal treatment, so blinding is imperfect and disclosed. Reviewers cite run/turn/seq and use locked rubric. A separate adjudicator resolves disagreements against recorded native evidence. Lock grades before revealing arm mapping. Same-model reviewers are not independent-vendor proof. They never determine a level-up or victory from reasoning text.

Retain prompt/source/context hashes, replay receipts, full delivered messages/images, calls/results, raw native state, supervisor events, seeds, model identity, timestamps, token/occupancy costs, reviewer outputs and adjudications. Missing native proof is unverified, not assumed success. No narrative summaries replacing the underlying trace.

Frozen validation and what a winner means

After nomination freeze exact wording, eligibility, data split, checkpoint/context hashes, caps, seed schedule, scoring code, controls, analysis and all flags. Then qualify held-out eligibility without feeding reserve content to the prompt planner. If no two eligible families, preserve the reserve and report blocked transfer validation. Same family repeated with fresh seeds is reliability at that start, not additional independent family transfer.

Target validation: on each of2held-out families,4baseline+4nominee episodes with randomized order and fresh draws; same state/history per family and equal executor allocation: for each held-out family, each GPU runs2baseline+2nominee episodes. Each readiness control runs once per arm per GPU. Freeze target and readiness batches together. No wording changes, outcome peeks, optional stopping or replacement of an inconvenient family based on success. Validate full batches even if initial outcomes look decisive. Safety/infrastructure aborts produce an incomplete campaign, never a winner.

Provisional campaign winner: nominee recovery≥6/8, at least3/4 in each held-out family, at least3more recoveries than baseline (≥37.5percentage-point observed lift), all4readiness episodes passed without candidate-only regression, no unresolved grading/state mismatch, and no lower observed native win count than baseline. Otherwise report no winner/inconclusive. This is a predeclared practical nomination rule, not statistical confirmation or a reliable success-rate estimate.

Confirmed improvement: requires another, separately approved, power-sized fixed comparison on fresh families/checkpoints and appropriate controls. This campaign does not promise one. There is no measured baseline for this recovery endpoint, and the minimum worthwhile population lift is still an operator judgment; use+25percentage points as a proposal, with+15/+50point sensitivity. Native victory confirmation remains distinct; a prompt meeting recovery criteria cannot be called a confirmed gym-winning improvement.

Report stratified exact randomization evidence, equal-weight family rates and intervals, plus pooled Fisher descriptively. With2families×2GPUs and2baseline+2nominee assignments per block, the conditional assignment space is6⁴=1296. Randomize independently within these four blocks; freeze that exact schedule and use the corresponding two-sided blocked randomization test; never apply pooled Fisher as though family and device design did not exist. Report statistical evidence descriptively for this underpowered validation; do not retroactively relabel it confirm-tier because a favorable pvalue appears. With two families, population transfer uncertainty is especially large. No boot/full-game promotion from checkpoint data.
