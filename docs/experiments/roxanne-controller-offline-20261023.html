Scoring the behavior, not the promise

Strict recovery requires a real exit from the gym, an observed capability change outside it, and a new Roxanne battle with that change, before any unchanged rematch. Levels, evolution, movesets and new party members are separate categories. Healing, reordered party/move slots, deletion without acquiring a move, narration, and walking back into the gym do not count. Outside acquisition is tied to the particular capability retained at rematch; a discarded gain cannot justify an unrelated change inside the gym.

Eventual recovery remains a separate diagnostic when an earlier unchanged retry occurred. Native victory is scored independently. Evidence gaps cannot become verified success, and events beyond the 210-decision / 30-minute episode cap cannot rescue its score.
