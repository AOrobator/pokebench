Blocked batch results

When the model presses one direction several times and the player does not move, the press result now says the player was blocked, where it is standing, which way it faces, and what the readout shows on the next tile. Today the same press reads as "batch or non-direction input", which names no cause.

1. What happened in control run 2919

Recorded in 2026-10-15T23-47-41_qwen36-gpu0_2919 (forensics bug 5). The player stood at (10,16) in Littleroot Town, facing left. The lab door warp is at (7,16), and (9,16) and (8,16) are wall. The model pressed LEFT, LEFT, LEFT to "walk left to (7,16)". Nothing moved. It got the same unhelpful result, and sent the same batch again: nine LEFT batches from (10,16) in turns 163–183, plus three DOWN×3 batches into the house row at turns 146–151.

Both lines are produced by replaying the recorded before and after states of seq 2432 (button_press before_state_seq 2422, tool_result state_seq 2431) through pressResult with the run's own switches, and with this switch added.

Off: unchanged world position/facing (batch or non-direction input; intermediate effects unknown)

On:  blocked: LEFT ×3 did not move you; you are still at (10,16) facing left. The tile to the left, (9,16), is a collision tile (# on the local map), so LEFT cannot move you from here.

All twelve recorded cases reproduce their delivered text byte for byte with the switch off, and all twelve change with it on. The tests replay them from packages/agent/test-support/blocked-batch-2919.json.

4. What it names, and in what order

The blocker comes only from the before-state's navigation_grid (the tiles the local map is drawn from), its object and warp lists, world_context.objects on the same map, and topology. The first match wins. Terrain causes end with "so LEFT cannot move you from here". A person, object or warp can change, so those say only what is there. With nothing shown, the head word is no movement, not blocked.

Facts the model is not shown stay out. With live-map-v1 off no tile is named, and the result is just no movement: LEFT ×3 did not move you; you are still at (10,16) facing left. With live-player-position-v1 off, coordinates and facing are dropped (you are still on the same tile, The tile to the left is …).

5. Game grounding

  • Collision: pokeemerald GetCollisionAtCoords refuses a step onto a tile whose collision bits are set, onto an object (CheckForObjectEventCollision), or across an elevation mismatch.
  • Elevation: IsElevationMismatchAt. Elevation 0 (stairs) and 15 (bridges) never mismatch; any other pair of different heights does. The player's height is navigation_grid.elevation. This is the same rule navigation uses (GAME_RULE, now in tile-rules.ts).
  • Ledges: MB_JUMP_EAST/WEST/NORTH/SOUTH (0x38–0x3B) hop only in their own direction. Edge walls: MetatileBehavior_Is*Blocked (0x30–0x37, 0xBE, 0xC0, 0xC1). Water: the surfable behaviors navigation already treats as water.
  • These tables moved unchanged from navigation.ts to tile-rules.ts so both features read one copy; navigation behavior is unchanged (all its tests pass unmodified).
