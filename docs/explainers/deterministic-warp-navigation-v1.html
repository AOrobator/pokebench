Deterministic warp navigation

When a requested exit is one tile in a safely identical row, navigate_to can take the shortest one. If the player already stands on a directional warp, it sends the required direction once and checks that the map changed.

The lab case

The two south-arrow tiles in Professor Birch's lab share the same complete destination, behavior and elevation. OFF preserves the requested right-hand coordinate. ON selects the reachable left-hand coordinate because its A* route is shorter, then performs the warp's DOWN press.

Exact equivalence gate

Must matchSame source map; all three destination fields, including destination warp ID; the source-known behavior; and target elevation.
Must be reachableEach candidate is planned with the same A* options. Route length wins; y then x breaks an equal-cost tie.
Fail closedDynamic destination 0x7F, missing or duplicate target data, unknown behavior, behavior mismatch, elevation mismatch, or no route keeps the requested coordinate.

One press, no retry

What the receipt says

With the switch on, the navigation receipt keeps target as the coordinate the model requested and adds selected_target for the coordinate actually chosen. Successful directional activation reports directional warp activated; inspect destination. If the map does not change, it reports the failed transition and stops.
