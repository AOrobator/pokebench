Disproved beliefs ledger

In run sh02 the model kept acting on beliefs the game had already contradicted: it looked for Professor Birch on the lab's tiles while every lab readout said he was not on the map and the scientist said he was away, and it pressed UP into the twin's script 15 times and LEFT into Birch's "Don't leave me" script 47 times. This switch keeps a short, factual ledger of such contradictions and shows it before every decision until the game state changes.

Nothing in an entry is shortened: every quote, every page and every turn is whole. The ledger names no goal and no route; it only pairs what the model went for with what the game showed.

Where entries come from

The push-back rule, on the twin's tile: the model pressed UP at (11,1); the pages began with the player at Q = (11,1); the call ended at R = (11,2). The step Q→R is DOWN, the opposite of a direction the model pressed, so the entry is "Moving UP from (11,1)". For navigate_to the rule is that R is closer than Q to where the walk began (Birch's Route 101 script: the walk began at (11,15), pages at (6,15), the call ended at (7,15)). Mom's TV cutscene, which moves the player while it talks but after a press of A only, is not a push-back.

The text rule is deliberately narrow. Names are only the readout's own story-character names, case-insensitive, where the game's PROF. also matches Prof. and Professor. The name must be followed in the same sentence by at (x,y) with no not, n't, never, no longer or away between. It must not be possessive, part of a sprite kind or inside parentheses.

Leaving the ledger

An entry is dropped as soon as it may no longer hold: any story flag in the recorded state changes (the game's own flags and variables, not the model's words), that map's readout lists the character as here, or the player is seen on the tile the push-back refused.
