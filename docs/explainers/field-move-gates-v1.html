Control run 2026-10-20T16-14-34_qwen36-gpu0_a8f9 looped in RUSTURF TUNNEL from turn 1282, calling navigate_to toward (29,16) and then (18,20). The map puts two BREAKABLE_ROCK objects at (24,4) and (24,5), the only way east. exit-reachability-v1's flood ignores objects, so both warps read foot, the untaken-exits line offered them, and navigate_to said "call navigate_to again to continue to (29,16)". With this switch one module owns every field-move and item gate, and the flood closes an obstacle whenever its gate is known closed.

navigate_to (29,16) with the switch on refuses before any press:

Navigation: (29,16) cannot be reached on foot from where you stand: every way to it on this map passes BREAKABLE_ROCK at (24,4) or (24,5), which needs a field move you cannot use yet; the game says: "It's a rugged rock, but a POKéMON may be able to smash it."; 0 ordinary button attempts.

A line says its obstacles "close every way on foot" to an exit only when they are that exit's whole blocker set, so closing exactly them cuts it off. When the ways pass obstacles of more than one kind, one line names them together, as from (30,25) in VICTORY ROAD B1F:

- PUSHABLE_BOULDER at (21,4) and BREAKABLE_ROCK at (18,12) together close every way on foot to (8,3): they need a field move you cannot use yet; the game says: "It's a big boulder, but a POKéMON may be able to push it aside." at the PUSHABLE_BOULDER and "It's a rugged rock, but a POKéMON may be able to smash it." at the BREAKABLE_ROCK

Other closed obstacle objects get a line of their own kind with no exits. Each line is checked against the flood with only its obstacles closed, on every map from every warp.

The wording follows surf-gated-exits-v2: it quotes the game's own text and says the way "needs a field move you cannot use yet" (or "an item you do not have yet"). It never names a badge, move or item.
