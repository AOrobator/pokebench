Loop supervisor

The loop guard keeps detecting and warning. Where it used to end the run as "stuck", it now posts a SUPERVISOR briefing and the run continues. Off by default; off keeps today's stop.

Why

In the 44 recent Think Fast runs, the guard warned 76 times and 56 of those loops broke afterwards. It also ended 20 runs, which is every "stuck" ending, and 9 of those runs had already left home after setting the clock. The two runs that reached the rival's house both used most of the 30-minute budget. Ending a run early cuts off exactly the search that might succeed.

Look back over your history, use recall_dialogue to check what characters told you, restate your current objective and the observed evidence for it, then try something you have not tried.

The recall_dialogue clause appears only when dialogue-log-v1 is on. The whole briefing stays under 2,500 characters. Each briefing is recorded as a loop_guard event with phase supervisor and the full text, so it shows in the timeline, Activity History and replay.
