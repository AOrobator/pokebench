## Executive summary

This experimental switch supplies authored speaker roles for the stolen-Goods chase and the later Devon employee request. Native message evidence identifies the exact source. Original dialogue and heuristic attribution remain recorded. Default OFF; no measured benefit or production activation is claimed.

## Recognition and delivery

\1. Coherent archived native state: matching US ROM, Rustboro map, owned ready field printer.

↓ exact source match

\2. Verified message pointer, script return address, ROM instructions and full buffer identity select one catalog entry.

↓ label already displayed pages

\3. Dialogue display names the authored speaker; enabled historical memory carries that role alongside the unchanged quote.

Wrong or missing evidence adds no label. The reader does not guess from the nearest person or reveal undisplayed pages from the loaded message.

## The two encounters

**Chase:** TEAM AQUA GRUNT says “Get out! Out of the way!”; DEVON EMPLOYEE says “Wait! Pleeeaaase!” and “Don’t take my GOODS!” The grunt and employee leave the scene.

**Later request:** DEVON EMPLOYEE recognizes the player from Petalburg Woods, describes the robbery and asks for the Devon Goods back. This is a separate source message, labeled only when observed.

A historical role does not establish the actor’s current presence, location or availability. Bystanders do not become participants simply because they were on screen.

## What persists

With observed instruction memory and unnamed-field retention enabled, the delivered request remains a whole historical episode. Labels change its presentation; existing raw attribution still drives selection and native retirement. JSON/legacy is capped at 16,000 characters; YAML at 19,000. Other settings can omit episodes; original recordings remain.

Healing or travel is not recovery. The existing recovery checkpoint uses native DEVON_GOODS_RECOVERED evidence, flag143. The label feature creates no active-task controller and supplies no new route or next-action advice.

## Independent switches

authored-scene-labels-v1 is globally OFF and OFF in both future recipe arms until separately enabled for research. Its labels preserve original speaker/on_screen fields, so the scene-follow-up reminder’s eligibility and one-reply lifetime are unchanged. Four probe arms can therefore compare neither, labels, reminder and both.
