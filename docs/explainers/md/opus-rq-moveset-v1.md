*Opus receives the prompt as the last message while the decision is due. It chooses and executes the action.*

## When it fires

Rain Badge held and Rayquaza in the party.

## What Opus sees

Aerial Ace over ExtremeSpeed and Waterfall over Rest.

## When it stops

The learned moves retire their instructions.

The prompt preserves Fly and Outrage. It only asks for a missing move when its TM or HM is in the bag. League entry ends the prompt.

## What the switch changes

ON adds this scoped prompt and records its activation in Activity History. OFF omits the prompt. A run with any of the four decisions enabled is classified as heavily assisted.
