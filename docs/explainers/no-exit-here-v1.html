No exit here

Runs invent exits and then walk into a map edge that leads nowhere: e046 wrote "Oldale's east exit to Petalburg" (s18744) and "Route 103's north exit to Rustboro" (s17437); deb4 read "SOUTH -> ROUTE116" and "EAST -> ROUTE119" for Littleroot (s11619); 9439 pressed DOWN on Littleroot's south edge and was told only "no movement" (s14790, s16266). With this switch on, that press or navigate_to call gets one line: there is no way off this map on that side, and here are the ways off it.

Every name comes from the result's own readout: the map from its Location line, connections and warps from its Map connections and warp lines (a two-tile door is one entry, "(1,0) or (2,0)"); a map the readout never named is given as its map id. With exit-reachability-v1 on, an exit its foot check leaves out is marked "(cannot be reached on foot from where you stand)". Nothing is clipped. No route or place the readout has not shown is named.
