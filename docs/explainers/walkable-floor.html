navigate_to fix · walkable floor

Sand, caves and mountain tops are floor, not walls

The map text has always drawn these tiles as ., but navigate_to kept its own short list of walkable tile types: plain ground, grass and doors. Anything else was a wall. On a beach or in a cave the tool could not take a single step. Now it uses the game's own rule: a tile without the collision bit is floor, unless the game does something special when you step on it.

Every one of these is drawn as . in the model's map. Before the fix only plain ground was walkable to navigate_to.

The new rule

This mirrors the game's own check for a step (GetCollisionAtCoords and CheckForObjectEventCollision). The tile's behavior only matters when the game does something special with it.

Only the highlighted box changed. Before, it was a short allow-list; now it is the game's short list of exceptions.

Forced movement0x20 0x40–0x48 0xBB 0xBC 0xD0 0xD2Ice, arrow and slide tiles, muddy slope, cracked floor, jump and spin mats move you on their own, so one press is no longer one tile.
Thin and cracked ice0x26 0x27Sootopolis gym ice cracks, then drops you.
Holes and step warps0x0D–0x0F 0x29 0x66Stepping on them warps or drops you.
One-way tiles0x30–0x37 0xBE 0xC0 0xC1Blocked from some sides only (0xBE is the secret-base breakable door). Still walls here; the chair thread handles them.
Ledges0x38–0x3FWalls when walking; the ledge-hops-v1 switch hops them in their arrow direction.
Acro Bike tiles0xD1 0xD3–0xD6Bumpy slope and rails refuse a walking player (CheckAcroBikeCollision).
