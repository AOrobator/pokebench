Winner gate

An experiment's result only nominates finalists. The finalists and the incumbent then run from boot, and only a finalist that wins those runs becomes the new incumbent. Nothing new starts until that is settled.

6. Records and controls

File (under runs/experiments/)Holds
<id>/nomination.jsonRanking, finalists and their switches, the finals incumbent preset, finals settings, reused incumbent runs.
<id>/finals.jsonlAppend-only launch / outcome / launcher_error for each finals run.
<id>/finals-result.jsonPer finalist: wins, ties and pairs, its share, pass/fail, and the winner.
promotions.jsonlOne line per declared winner. Their switches, in order, are the incumbent overlay.

The queue page shows a WINNER GATE card with the finalists, finals pips and verdicts, plus WITHDRAW FINALS and RESUME FINALS. A withdrawal stops before the next run and keeps new experiments waiting. An experiment can shorten its finals with finals: {runsPerArm, maxWallMs, target}. Setting POKEBENCH_WINNER_GATE=off restores the queue exactly as it was.
