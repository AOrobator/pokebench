## Architecture

Switch `ineffective-loops-v1` (guard, needs null, default off). `LoopDetector` 10th / `attachLoopGuard` 11th argument (after navigation-truth-v1's `messageTruth`, since the main merge at fd30cfae; 9th / 10th before it), wired from the FeatureSet in `live.ts` and `decision-probe.ts`.

- **(a) waiting observes.** `waitingState(state)`: outside battle, a ready active UI (no diagnostic), a ready naming screen, or a locked field holding a ready message (dismiss/button/page/scroll wait). Its fingerprint is the player's place and facing, the lock, the UI, naming, dialogue and message, story, party, money, badges and name. An observe whose waiting fingerprint equals the previous game call's counts as an unchanged observation (the existing counter, threshold 3 repeats), and its warning uses `WAITING_MESSAGE`, also for naming screens and BOY/GIRL, name and clock prompts that today's fingerprint already counts with the generic message (review round 1). A waiting state not among the last 16 recovers the episode. Field states with controls free keep today's fingerprint; scripts, transitions and printing text are the game acting and never count.
- **(b) few-target loops.** A move is a navigate_to or a press with a direction button, not an errored call, whose result is on the overworld outside battle with a known tile and no menu, naming screen or message, whose own start state is known with field controls free and nothing open (review round 1), and during which no message or dialogue appeared. Moves are kept per map (12 kept): any game call ending on another map starts them again (review round 1: also non-moves, such as stairs taken while a script held the controls); a battle, an open menu or naming screen, a conversation not heard since the moves began (new information, e.g. the nurse's heal) or a story milestone clears them; the same conversation again (the Maniac's line) only makes that call not a move. When the newest move's tile has ended 3 (`cycleRepeats`) moves, the loop is the moves from the first of those three to now: if they ended on at most 2 tiles and their targets alternate (one target named again after a different one), the guard raises a `cycle` warning with its own message (`REVISIT_MESSAGE`), then the supervisor briefing. Evidence (schema: at most 2): the first of the three moves and the call that completed the count. The episode recovers only on a tile not among the last 16 results, or a story milestone (as push-back episodes do); a turn or a step back onto a tile already seen does not end it.
- OFF: byte-identical (no waiting fingerprint, no moves, no place tracking beyond push-back's own).

Design notes (from the measurement below): the brief says "two to three" targets. The rule asks for alternation (a target named again after a different one) and at most two end tiles, with no upper bound on distinct targets. Without the alternation rule, an arrival followed by two bumps (960a T5–T7) or an arrival, a refused navigate_to and a turn toward a sign (4caa T479–T481) counted after three calls; without the conversation clear, a Pokémon Center heal (4caa T451–T456: walk to the counter, talk, walk back) counted. 4caa T202–T206 (five targets, no repeat, two tiles) does not count under the alternation rule; the same loop is caught at T212.
