From power-on to the Hall of Fame in 17 h 16 min, in one run with no reloads. It lost to Glacia on its first try at the Elite Four, bought supplies at the League shop and won the second try.

Claude Opus 5.5 played 4,124 turns. It caught Rayquaza at Sky Pillar with the Master Ball and took it into the League. The Rayquaza lane gives four one-time hints and a Mach Bike dash tool, so the run is marked (assisted) on the [leaderboard](results.html).

The run is released for checking: [run 67d7](runs/67d7.html) has the event log, memory snapshots, save states and the command that verifies them against your own copy of the game.
