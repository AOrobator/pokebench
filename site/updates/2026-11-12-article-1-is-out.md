The first article on PokéBench is out: [PokéBench: Harness Engineering for Long-Horizon Agents](https://medium.com/@andrew/pokébench-harness-engineering-for-long-horizon-agents-4b093bdfc93a). It covers what the harness does for the model each turn, why most runs stall, and what changed between the first runs and the Hall of Fame.

The model traces behind it are released on GitHub, one thinking file and one full trace per run, linked from every run on the [runs page](runs.html).
